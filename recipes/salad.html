Fresh & Authentic Greek Salad

A traditional Greek salad (Horiatiki) is a vibrant, refreshing dish that brings the flavors of the Mediterranean straight to your table. Packed with crisp cucumbers, juicy pot-ripe tomatoes, sharp red onions, Kalamata olives, and creamy feta cheese, it's finished with a dusting of oregano and a generous drizzle of high-quality extra virgin olive oil. Perfect as a light lunch or a side dish for grilled meats.

Ingredients

  • 3 large ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 cucumber, sliced into rounds or half-moons
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced into rings
  • 1/2 cup Kalamata olives, whole and pitted
  • 1 block (7 oz/200g) feta cheese
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • Red wine vinegar (optional, to taste)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Prep the Vegetables: Wash and chop the tomatoes, cucumber, and green pepper. Place them in a large salad bowl.
  2. Add Onions & Olives: Add the thinly sliced red onion and Kalamata olives to the bowl.
  3. Season: Sprinkle a pinch of sea salt and dried oregano over the vegetable mixture. Remember that feta and olives are salty, so be careful with the added salt.
  4. Dress: Drizzle the extra virgin olive oil and a splash of red wine vinegar over the salad. Toss gently to combine.
  5. Add the Feta: Place the block of feta cheese on top of the salad (or crumble it if preferred).
  6. Finish: Sprinkle more oregano and a drizzle of olive oil over the feta. Serve immediately with crusty bread.
