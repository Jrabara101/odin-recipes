World's Best Lasagna

Description

This is a classic, meaty lasagna recipe that has been perfected over generations. It features layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella.

It's the ultimate comfort food for Sunday dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sweet Italian sausage
  • 1/2 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and sausage in a large skillet until browned.
  2. Add tomatoes and seasonings; simmer for 30 minutes.
  3. Boil the lasagna noodles until al dente.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with the egg.
  5. Layer sauce, noodles, and cheese mixture in a baking dish.
  6. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45 minutes.
