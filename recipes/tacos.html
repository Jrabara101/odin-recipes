Classic Mexican Street Tacos

Experience the authentic taste of Mexico with these classic Street Tacos. Small, soft corn tortillas filled with perfectly seared carne asada (marinated skirt steak), topped simply with fresh cilantro, diced white onions, and a squeeze of lime. These tacos are bite-sized explosions of flavor, perfect for a fiesta or a quick weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

The Meat (Carne Asada)

  • 1 lb skirt steak or flank steak, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

The Fillings & Tortillas

  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/2 cup white onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • Salsa verde or roja (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Marinate the Steak: In a bowl, mix soy sauce, lime juice, minced garlic, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Add the chopped steak and toss to coat. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes (or up to 4 hours in the fridge).
  2. Cook the Meat: Heat a skillet or griddle over high heat with a little oil. Add the marinated steak (discarding excess marinade) and cook for 5-7 minutes until seared and cooked through.
  3. Warm the Tortillas: While the meat rests, heat the corn tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame for about 30 seconds per side until warm and slightly charred. Stack them in a clean kitchen towel to keep warm.
  4. Assemble: To serve, double up the tortillas (use two per taco). Spoon a generous amount of meat into the center.
  5. Garnish: Top with diced onions, fresh cilantro, and a squeeze of fresh lime juice. Add salsa if desired.
  6. Serve: Enjoy hot immediately!
